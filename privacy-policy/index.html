Privacy Policy - OmniBase

Last Updated: 08-08-2025

OmniBase, developed and maintained exclusively by DOTIX Technologies Private Limited, is a unified Backend as a Service (BaaS) platform intended solely for internal use within our organization.
This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect information when you access or use OmniBase as part of internal operations.

1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies only to OmniBase’s internal usage by authorized personnel of DOTIX Technologies Private Limited. It does not apply to public-facing websites, applications, or services unless explicitly stated.

2. Information We Collect Automatically

With your permission, we may collect the following categories of information for operational, diagnostic, and security purposes:

  • Device & Account Information: Model, OS version, device ID, network details.
  • Log Data: IP address, crash reports, usage patterns, or login activity.
  • Location Data: GPS, Wi-Fi, and cell tower details for location-based offers or security checks.
  • Cookies & Tracking Technologies: Used for analytics, personalization, and performance optimization.
  • Installation Data: App version, update status, and related metadata.

3. Information You Provide

We may collect information you provide voluntarily when using OmniBase internally, including but not limited to:

  • Account credentials for authentication.
  • Files and data stored in the system.
  • Administrative configurations and settings.

4. How We Use the Information

We process collected information strictly for internal operational purposes, including:

  • Ensuring secure and authorized access to OmniBase services.
  • Monitoring performance and diagnosing technical issues.
  • Improving backend efficiency and optimizing features.
  • Enforcing internal policies and security protocols.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or share collected data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data may be shared internally within DOTIX Technologies for operational needs or:

  • Legal Compliance: When required by law or valid legal request.
  • Security Incidents: To investigate, prevent, or address security threats.
  • Internal Development: To improve system capabilities and performance.

6. Data Security

We implement enterprise-grade security measures, including encryption, role-based access control, and continuous monitoring, to safeguard collected data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

7. Data Retention

Data is retained only for as long as required to fulfill operational and legal purposes. When data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

8. Your Rights (Internal Users)

As an internal user, you may request:

  • Access to the information stored about you.
  • Correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion of data no longer necessary for operational purposes.

Requests can be directed to the internal IT administrator.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our operations or legal requirements. All updates will be communicated internally.
